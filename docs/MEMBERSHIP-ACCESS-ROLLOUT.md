# Free and Premium access
Free/guest: fixed preview up to 24 products each in business, women and men collections; filters and sort operate on that fixed subset. Direct product routes and saved-product/supplier detail APIs are scoped; free customers cannot use keyword or product-link search. Account and cart remain available to all, real purchase remains disabled. Premium and complimentary owner: full catalogue and catalogue keyword search; read-only saved marketplace link lookups. Owner-only live discovery/purchase pilot remains unchanged until provider permissions, privacy and durable quotas are verified. Public live search by link, keyword/image and purchasing are disclosed as in rollout, not available. Existing Stripe subscription price/renewal unchanged. Raw catalogue JSON currently resides in public assets; separate migration to server-only storage is required for comprehensive data access control. Product purchasing is still blocked for all public users.
