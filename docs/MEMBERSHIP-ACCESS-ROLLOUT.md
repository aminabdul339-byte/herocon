# Free and Premium access
Free/guest: fixed preview up to 72 products (three 24-product pages) each in business, women and men collections; category previews independently capped at six business or eight personal products on desktop, with fewer visible on smaller screens to preserve two rows; direct product access is the union of these fixed samples; filters and sort operate on that fixed subset. Direct product routes and saved-product/supplier detail APIs are scoped; free customers cannot use keyword or product-link search. Account and cart remain available to all, real purchase remains disabled. Premium and complimentary owner: full catalogue and catalogue keyword search; read-only saved marketplace link lookups. Owner-only live discovery/purchase pilot remains unchanged until provider permissions, privacy and durable quotas are verified. Public live search by link, keyword/image and purchasing are disclosed as in rollout, not available. Existing Stripe subscription price/renewal unchanged. Known raw catalogue JSON download routes are redirected to membership; existing server filesystem imports are unchanged. This is not DRM and cannot revoke previously copied data. Review additional public assets and any external repository visibility before launch. Product purchasing is still blocked for all public users.
