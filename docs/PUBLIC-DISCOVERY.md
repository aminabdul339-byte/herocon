# Premium discovery

The image is sent to Hiobuy for matching. Public uncached calls (including detail and paging) are limited to 10 per account and 20 shared per UTC day by immutable private Blob claims. Cache hits do not spend slots. Provider quotas still apply across all jobs and owner requests. Conservative launch cap; raise after verifying plan and usage. Provider 403/429 errors are displayed, never bypassed. Procurement, payments, shipping and Taobao live access unchanged.
